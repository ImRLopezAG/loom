## Full-suite challenges and retained limits

**Capacity is a deliberate fail-closed bound, not proven sufficient for this suite.** `protocol.md:23,41-45` keeps 256 normal retained lifetimes, up to 4096 emergency acquisition attempts/handles plus root, 64 trace files/8 MiB total, 1 MiB per helper file and 4096 ordinary events with terminal reserve. The full suite has CLI, packed-consumer and nested build processes; low simultaneous occupancy does not prevent cumulative lifetime exhaustion. There is no measured full-suite population or guarantee that these bounds permit both target fixtures to finish. A cap hit must retain available evidence, perform bounded owned cleanup, label incompleteness and stop; it cannot trigger automatic cap increases or a rerun. Sampled peaks remain lower bounds. Neither 124 files nor the historical 89 file workers is a simultaneous-process count. Lower OS descriptor limits can fail before the designed capacity.

**Report parsing must remain distinct from child status.** `protocol.md:37,45,47` separates Bun integration from the Vitest cap/reporter, streams the original workload console under existing GitHub masking, keeps only a finite private raw capture, and demands explicit unavailable/ambiguous totals. Turbo output and nested consumers can contain multiple summaries; a last-match or selected-case-one-count parser from the old supervisor is insufficient. The expected 279 cases/124 files and two named skips are reconciliation expectations, never selection changes or defaults for missing output. Exact reporter introspection remains unavailable unless independently established; historical passing-log suppression supplies a mechanism, not historical resolved configuration.

**Partial fixture progression is expected on the target red.** `protocol.md:25-33,47-49` separates valid syntax/prefix, instrumentation coverage, fixture lifecycle, workload outcome and restoration. A credential timeout can produce recovery polls/wait-end/cleanup without any obsolete/intermediate/latest-generation stages. A provisioning timeout can leave an indexed start/spawn with no exit or later indices. Those missing stages cannot erase validated earlier evidence or become zeros/success. Conversely, a finally wait-end/await-end does not prove a successful predicate/join. An ordinary red with safe cleanup/restoration still gets an attempted synthetic receipt publication.

**Correlation has finite reach.** Event IDs describe async ancestry, which can remain stale across a later coordinator loop. Root/coordinator/revision and version domains are authoritative. Unscoped overlapping preparation remains ambiguous; missing post-assertion sync-end cannot distinguish synchronization failure from cancellation at admission. Watch events do not prove exhaustive OS delivery. PID-only trace fields cannot disambiguate reuse or unobserved births; attribution must remain unknown. Missing child-exit is not a deadlock diagnosis.

**Costs and durability remain qualified.** Measured cost excludes close, pre-call hashes and other work outside its timing span. Partial writes/pairs, truncation and I/O failure produce incomplete evidence. Completed append writes are not fsync/power-loss durability. A diagnostic green may reflect observer effects and cannot establish the original cause or a fix.

## Exit, restoration, publication and authority

`protocol.md:39-49` preserves the original workload exit independently of diagnostic status. An original nonzero status always yields a nonzero wrapper. An original zero permits wrapper zero only with complete protocol and restoration. A signal/unknown status cannot be fabricated as success; root wait-status ownership is essential to preserve that distinction. Upload success cannot alter the original integration outcome, and ordinary later steps remain blocked after red or incomplete supervision.

Preimages include exact bytes/modes for seven existing files, helper absence and a verified dist backup; the applied flag precedes the first mutation. All diagnostic source/config/dist mutations are restored only after owned descendants are established terminal. **Unknown or failed cleanup blocks restoration**, because rewriting source/dist beneath potentially live descendants would invalidate both ownership and evidence. Known zero children after forced cleanup still does not make the diagnostic complete. Unrelated generated outputs are inventoried, unexpected tracked drift fails, and no blanket checkout/reset/clean is permitted. Actual before/after hashes and initial-diff comparison remain required.

Only reconstructed, strictly validated synthetic records and finite receipts may enter publish; raw console, backups, SQL, credentials and arbitrary paths remain outside it. Final file/link/descriptor/size/count bounds and atomic publication must be implemented and freshly reviewed. The pinned upload action targets only publish, retains three days, excludes hidden files and errors on missing files. These are design obligations, not an assertion that an absent executable implements them.

The 1800-second supervisor ceiling does not extend the existing 30-minute job wall limit; prior steps consume that budget. Two-second TERM/KILL phases are emergency ownership cleanup, not extensions of application/test deadlines. Cancellation, SIGKILL, runner loss, filesystem failure or incomplete ownership can prevent restoration and receipt upload. No broad service/PG termination, database reset or unconditional browser progression is authorized.
