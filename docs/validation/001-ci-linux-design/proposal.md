## Durable output and perturbation

Trace files are ordinary append-only files in a unique runner-temporary directory OUTSIDE the watched fixture/checkout. They are independent of console reporters. Per helper instance: 4096 stage events plus their paired cost events, at most 1 MiB; records at most 4096 bytes; reserve 8192 bytes for final event/cost. O_NOFOLLOW, 0600, complete synchronous writes and close per event. A bounded trace.truncated marker disables subsequent output. A fixed IO/close failure marker disables the helper; no raw error is printed. The supervisor must treat missing expected markers, truncation, malformed JSON, any IO failure, excess files or failed cleanup as invalid/incomplete diagnostic evidence, irrespective of test exit.

"Durable" means completed writes retained in files for artifact upload after test failure, not power-loss/fsync durability. Synchronous open/write/close and SHA256/UUID creation add overhead; AsyncLocalStorage and extra promise .then/.finally observers can change scheduling. trace.cost measures open/write work but excludes close, pre-call hashes, extra microtasks and helper import. No zero-overhead or uninstrumented equivalence claim is allowed. A passing diagnostic does not prove absence of the original race. A new timeout may be observer-induced. No clock subtraction or deadline compensation.
